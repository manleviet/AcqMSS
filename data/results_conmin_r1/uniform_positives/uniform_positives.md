## Design

- **Sampler**: exact uniform over sol(Cτ ∪ root) by top-down SDD sampling (pysdd compilation as in
  `exact_sdd_model_counter.py`; element choice ∝ exact big-integer model counts; unconstrained
  variables uniform). Every drawn configuration is checked against Cτ.
- **Runs** per fold (same folds, same training negatives, same held-out test set): `original` (the
  fold's own positives — the gate), `uniform_n`, `uniform_2n`, `uniform_4n` (n = n_train_pos of the
  fold), `mixed_n` (original positives + the same n uniform ones). Seed `82 + fold`; draws are
  nested (the n sample is a prefix of the 2n and 4n samples), so for a given fold index and n the
  uniform positives are identical across samplings.
- **Learning**: library path (prepare_task, raw negatives; `acquire_pool_and_cover`; `finish_kb` at
  k = 1; ConMin's `Reduce` over A in the ablation's order). Scoring: `reduce_a_ablation._score`
  (all tiers, root in the premise, P_std; sol P/R by exact counting). Spurious, classes and dead
  features as in `../dead_features/dead_features.md`.
- **Scope on KB₄** (decided 2026-10-26, CPU): `original` and `uniform_n` only; `uniform_2n`,
  `uniform_4n`, `mixed_n` not measured (one fold of 696 uniform positives took > 2 h of Stage 1).
  The single KB₄ `uniform_4n` probe (rs_1n fold 0) is listed as partial and not aggregated.
- **2-COV**: n_train_pos = 0, so every variant equals `original`.
- **ε(n)** = (ln|B| + ln 20)/n with n = positives used (δ = 0.05); under uniform D the D-mass of the
  region Reduce(A) rejects is 1 − sol R. The bound speaks only to uniform positives; the
  `original` and `mixed_n` rows are shown for contrast.

## Notes

1. **KB₄ uniform runs lose soundness through one target clause.** Reduce(A) learned from uniform
   positives misses exactly target constraint 14 (`¬Previouslyvisitedpages ∨ Locallyvisitedpages ∨
   Externalreferringpages`) on 15 of 17 consistent folds (sol P .927–.954; the other 2 keep it,
   sol P 1.00). The clause has no bias counterpart (Step 4, note 2): uniform positives refute the
   stronger spurious *requires* that stood in for it, so nothing entails it any more.
2. **Cost.** Stage 1 grows faster than linearly in the number of uniform positives (KB₄ rs_1n f0:
   original 174 → 414 s; uniform 174 → 844 s; uniform 696 → > 2 h).
3. **ε(n) is exceeded** (fold means: measured 1 − sol R > ε) only in low thirds: KB₃ `uniform_2n` (.820 vs .554)
   and KB₄ `uniform_n` (.666 vs .630); ε > 1 (vacuous) for KB₁ low and KB₃ low at n. Everywhere else
   the measured mass is below the bound, by 1–2 orders of magnitude in the high thirds.

sol R in the high thirds from ≤ .70 to .87 / .99 / 1.00 / 1.00 (KB₁–KB₄; KB₄ high 2.7e-6 → 1.00) and in

floor of ~6–9 spurious R∖C constraints survives even 4n uniform positives on KB₂/KB₃.
